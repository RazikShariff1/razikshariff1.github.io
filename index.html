About

I’m a backend engineer (SDE 1) at Zopping in Bengaluru, building Go and PostgreSQL microservices for a multi-seller e-commerce marketplace. I work on the parts where orders, stock and money change hands: order processing, inventory, payments and dispatch.

I like systems that stay correct when things go wrong, such as a retried request that must not create a second order, or two shoppers racing for the last item in stock.

What I’m doing

  • Order Processing

    Idempotent order placement and a tracked lifecycle from pending to packed, dispatched and completed.

  • Inventory & Logistics

    Expiring stock reservations so concurrent checkouts never oversell, released on cancel.

  • Payments

    Payment status tracking across paid, cancelled and refunded orders, with cash on delivery settled on completion.

  • Platform & Access

    An API gateway with seller-scoped, role-based access in front of every service.
